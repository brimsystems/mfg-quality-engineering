S3 A3

F-14 scrap: root cause

Precision machining shop, about 150 employees, IATF 16949 and AS9100, one plant. January 2024 to December 2025; family F-14, 4140 bar, multi-axis turned.
Corrective action record; DMAIC project.

Background and problem

MT-04 carried 43% of the scrap on family F-14 and is named on the scrap report as the cause. The family scrapped 3.6% of 70,605 pieces before the change against 1.7% on F-12, the same material and route.

Current condition

Share of the family's scrap pieces and of its lots by machine, 76 lots before the change.

Target

Family scrap rate below 1.0% on every bar lot, held through 2026.

Analysis

Scrap rate by bar hardness band for each insert grade.
  • MT-04 with bar hardness and insert grade in the model: odds ratio 1.04, p = 0.74.
  • Bar above 32 HRC with the standard insert: 11.3% scrap against 0.8 to 1.0% in the other three cells.
  • That combination explains 69% of the between-lot scrap variance.
  • MT-04 ran 38% of its lots on bar above 32 HRC against 21% on the other machines.

Results

Scrap rate 3.55% before, 0.87% on the 25 lots of the confirmation window (p < 0.001) and 0.92% on all 36 lots since; F-12 unchanged at 1.72% and 1.60%. Scrap cost $4,196 a month before, $766 since.
