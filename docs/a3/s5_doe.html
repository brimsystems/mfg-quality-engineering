S5 A3

Surface finish: designed experiment

Precision machining shop, about 150 employees, IATF 16949 and AS9100, one plant. January 2024 to December 2025; Ra in micrometres.
PPAP element 11 supporting data; DMAIC project.

Background and problem

Ra on C-16180-28 (part P-16180-A, 316 stainless) ran at 1.41 µm on 44 lots against an upper limit of 1.6 µm: index 0.96, 2,006 ppm expected above the limit.

Current condition

Lot mean Ra by start date with the upper limit and the prediction interval at the chosen settings.

Target

Ra at or below 0.76 µm with the index against the 1.6 µm limit at or above 1.33, held through 2026.

Analysis

Main effects and the feed by nose radius interaction, 16 runs.
  • Feed by nose radius: effect -0.405 µm, p < 0.001.
  • Feed effect +0.59 µm at the 0.4 mm radius and -0.22 µm at 0.8 mm.
  • Prediction at the chosen settings 0.76 µm, interval 0.61 to 0.91.
  • The alias with speed by coolant is resolved by production at the old settings: 1.41 µm against 1.40 and 0.99.

Countermeasures

ActionOwnerWhen
Feed 0.20 mm/rev and 0.8 mm nose radius written into the routing and the setup sheetManufacturing engineerin production from 12 August 2025; documents March 2026

Results

Ra 0.700 µm on 11 lots since 12 August 2025, every lot inside the prediction interval; 4 of 4 confirmation runs inside it; index 5.65 against the limit, from 0.96.
